What would count as progress?

Finding that millions or billions of numbers reach 1 is evidence, but it is not a proof.

The interesting goal is to discover a statement such as: 

For every positive integer n,
some explicitly defined transformation T(n)
produces a smaller positive integer.

If such a statement could be proved for all integers, it could potentially reduce the Collatz problem to a well-founded descent.
